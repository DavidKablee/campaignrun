# C90 — Meet anyone: rooms you can switch, a national square, friends

Mobilise was a handful of strangers who happened to land together. This lets a player go where the people are, meet players from any state, and keep the ones they like.

## What changed
- **Rooms list** (header button): every open room with its place, how many are in it (of 12) and how many are your friends. **Quick switch** jumps to the fullest room with space. `GET /api/places/rooms` returns places and counts only, never names.
- **Naija Square** (`naija:square`): one national place, in the kind row and the Rooms list. It is no state and adds nothing to a state's Board. Same caps and rules as any room. Scenery borrows the stadium look (`places.square.scenery`).
- **Join a specific room**: `join` takes an optional `roomId`; used only if it is the same place and has space, otherwise the player lands in another room and is told ("That room was full, so you are in another one"). Invites can carry a room number (`#mobilise=kano.market.3`).
- **Rooms top up first**: a new join goes to the fullest room that still has space, and a new room opens only when every room of that place is full.
- **Friends** (`place_friends`): tap a player, Add friend; they Accept (or Not now) from People, Friends. Up to 50 friends and 50 pending. A friend's place shows only to accepted friends, with a Join button. Remove covers decline, cancel, unfriend and unblock. Block removes the link both ways and shows the blocked person nothing. Asking each other at once makes you friends at once. A mutual request needs no typed names.
- **Mutes** are kept on the device by board name and hold across rooms and days.
- **Reconnect**: a player who drops comes back to where they were standing (within `rooms.rejoinSeconds`).
- **Rally with only Locals near** pays half (`soloPercent`) and adds no Board activity, so a solo player is no longer locked out.
