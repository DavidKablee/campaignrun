- **Landmarks** (`data/states/delta.json`, all `verified: false`): Niger Bridge (Asaba head), Sapele Timber Wharf, Effurun Roundabout, Warri Stadium, Ogheye Floating Market (fallback in the plan: Ogbe-Ogonogo Market). - **New styles:** lots `sawmill-shed`, `plank-lockups`; kerbside `logs`, `planks`, `jerrycans`, `sachets`.
