# C43 — the page where you dress a Contender
Status: done      Depends on: C38, C40
## Goal
Make the look editor readable and usable on a phone. It was neither: half its controls were
invisible, and the half you could see pushed the buttons off the bottom of the screen.
## Done when
Every control is legible on the white card, the editor is reachable without scrolling past a
biography, the action bar cannot leave the viewport, and the screen says which state it is sending
you to.

## What was wrong

**You could not read the clothes.** C38 turned `.cr-studio-panel` into a white card and left every
control inside it painted for the dark ground it used to sit on. Seven garment names, the back link
and both perk chips were `--cr-paper` cream on `--cr-surface` white. The only option you could make
out was the selected one, by its outline — so you picked clothes by tapping blanks and watching the
model change. This is the same failure as C40's `.cr-daily-chip`: a colour that was correct before
the chrome moved and was never revisited.

**The buttons were off the screen.** `.cr-screen` is `flex: 1` inside `.cr-shell` with no
`min-height`, and a flex item's automatic minimum is its content, so the studio grew to 1072px on an
896px phone. The grid under it then made it worse — `grid-template-rows: … 1fr` is
`minmax(auto, 1fr)`, so the panel track refused to shrink below its content either. Reset and
Campaign sat 176px below the fold with nothing to scroll.

**The button named the wrong state.** It said "Campaign in Lagos" on all 37 states, the same bug the
roster's state pill had before C40.

Smaller things: twelve colour swatches sat under the options with nothing saying what they painted
(on the Headwear tab they read as a second palette); Reset was offered to a player who had changed
nothing; and nothing said whether dressing a Contender changes how they play.

## Shipped

The screen moved to `ui/StudioScreen.tsx`, which is where screens live since C40, and its one piece
of real logic moved to `src/studio.ts`, where it can be tested: `changedSlots(base, current)` —
which of the four slots differ from the Contender's own look, counting a recolour as a change,
because recolouring a shirt you kept is the first edit most players make.

That one function pays for three things: a dot on each tab you have edited, a Reset that is disabled
until there is something to reset (and says how many slots it will undo), and a label that reads
"Original look" when you have not touched anything.

The rest:

- Controls on the surface palette — white pills with ink text, green fill for the selected one.
- Perk and trade-off as two compact labelled facts instead of two invisible chips. Compact on
  purpose: as full cards they filled the scroller on a 390×844 phone and pushed the editor itself
  below the fold.
- Swatches labelled with the slot they paint ("Top colour"), in an auto-fitting grid.
- The back link moved out of the panel and onto the stage, where cream is legible and it costs no
  panel height.
- One scroller (`.cr-studio-scroll`) with `flex: none` children, and the action bar outside it, so
  the campaign button is always on screen. `min-height: 0` on the screen and `minmax(0, 1fr)` on the
  panel track are what stop the card growing past the shell.
- Reset moved out of the action bar and in with the controls it undoes. Two buttons down there cost
  a third of the scroller on a phone, and side by side they either wrapped the campaign button to
  two lines or clipped the state name off it — the part this button was getting wrong to begin with.
- The stage's 45° barber-pole overlay became a soft floor glow. It was noise behind a character
  whose own stripes are the point.
- One line of fineprint (`copy.studioLooks`) answering the two questions the page never did: the
  outfit stays on this device, and it does not change how the Contender plays.

## Tests
`apps/game/test/studio.test.ts` (7): nothing changed on an untouched Contender, a swapped garment, a
recolour of a garment you kept, the same hex in another case counting as unchanged, every changed
slot reported rather than the first, and the slot table pointing at option lists that exist in
content. 240 tests (was 233).

Measured in Chrome at 414×896, 390×844 and 1280×800: the panel ends inside the viewport at all
three, the scroller scrolls, and the action bar is the last thing on screen. The e2e smoke flow,
which clicks through this screen, still passes.

## Open
- The character faces the camera but the headtie still renders as a flat slab from some angles —
  a model issue from the C21/C33 track, not a layout one.
- The option lists are text only. Thumbnails would make the editor skimmable, but the renderer has
  no per-garment preview and generating 23 of them is its own chunk.
- Nothing here is testable in the browser from CI: the e2e suite checks the screen renders and that
  one garment can be clicked, not that the text is legible on its background. A contrast assertion
  would have caught this in C38.
