# C68 — The known gaps, closed where they are client-only

## What
- **Radio callouts** (`radioCallouts.ts`, `App.tsx`): lines no longer name a ride, a religious phrase or another state. Weave and close-call lines are neutral; "Lagos" lines only play for Lagos; zone flair is "South-South convoy", "Coast road", "Middle Belt convoy"; `calloutForEvent` and `calloutForLandmark` take the state name.
- **Barrier obstacle** (`render/props.ts`): the red/white rails, twin lamps and red warning triangle that read as a checkpoint are now a yellow/black chevron board on grey A-frame legs (the lamps stay). `warnSign` is removed.
- **Hazard atmosphere** (`render/hazardAtmosphere.ts`, wired through `RenderOpts.hazards` and `World`): dust-plume, dust-storm, heat-shimmer, river-fog, low-visibility, rain-slick, flash-flood and flood-sheet now tint the fog; dust and storm also switch the drifting dust on; the fog far plane is shortened by at most 25% and never below 70 m. Client-only: the hazards' numbers are untouched.
- **Swipes from the pads** (`input/gestures.ts`, `controls.ts`, `App.tsx`): a swipe that starts on BEAT or PERK now steers (the right thumb rests there). A still press on a pad is not a tap or a horn. PERK now fires on release of a press that stayed put, so a swipe that begins on it does not spend the charge; BEAT still fires on press (it is timing-critical and a no-op when no beat window is open).
- **Dev API address** (`api/client.ts`, `api/src/app.ts`, `.env`): with `VITE_API_URL` unset the client calls the host that served the page on port 8787, so a phone on the LAN reaches the laptop's API. In non-production the API accepts private-LAN origins (192.168.x, 10.x, 172.16–31.x) in addition to `CR_ALLOWED_ORIGINS`. The stale `VITE_API_URL` line in `.env` is commented out.
