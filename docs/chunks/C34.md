# C34 — The remaining 28 state packs
Status: done      Depends on: C08 (state packs), C27 (batch 2)
## Goal
Nine states had real content; 28 of the calendar's 37 entries were schema-ready stubs, so every
unlock week from 9 Nov onward would have opened onto nothing. Author the rest.
## Done when
`pnpm verify` green; every id in `calendar.unlocks` has a pack that parses, its `audioStem` matches
`states-index.json`, its `signatureRide` is a real ride, and no landmark is a palace or named after
a person.
## Shipped
### The packs
All 28 remaining states, each with five landmarks, a palette, scenery, hazards, a signature ride
and a welcome line: **enugu, katsina, ogun, plateau** (9 Nov) · **bauchi, akwa-ibom, imo, sokoto**
(16 Nov) · **osun, benue, adamawa, edo** (23 Nov) · **abia, jigawa, ondo** (30 Nov) · **niger,
gombe, cross-river** (7 Dec) · **ebonyi, kebbi, ekiti** (14 Dec) · **kogi, taraba, bayelsa**
(21 Dec) · **nasarawa, yobe, zamfara** (28 Dec) · **fct** (4 Jan, finale).

No loader or application code was needed for the packs themselves — `import.meta.glob` picks up a
new file, as C08 intended. First-load gzip is unchanged at 330.6 KB; each pack is its own ~0.5 KB
lazy chunk.

Three `biomeKit` values had never been used by any pack and now are: `rainforest` (8 states),
`harmattan-dune` (Sokoto, Yobe) and `capital-city` (FCT). That matters for the BACKLOG item that
wants `biomeKit` wired into the Three.js world — every kit the enum declares now has real callers.

### Choices worth knowing about
- **No landmark is named after a person.** Nigerian stadiums, airports and parks are very often
  named after politicians, including serving ones, so naming a venue after anyone is a direct route
  into the thing `AGENTS.md` forbids. Every landmark here is a market, a geographic feature, a gate,
  a roundabout, a waterfront or a generic civic ground ("<City> Township Stadium"). No palaces or
  thrones either — spec amendment A8, now also covered by a test rather than only by the absence of
  `palace` from `venueTypeSchema`.
- **Welcome and farewell lines only use a language the state actually speaks.** Yoruba, Igbo and
  Hausa greetings are used where they apply; everywhere else the line is English or Pidgin, which is
  what the shipped Rivers and Delta packs already did. `farewellLine` is a new **optional** schema
  field for exactly this reason: 28 states have one, 9 deliberately do not and fall back to a
  neutral sign.
- **`hazards` is now a closed enum** (`hazardSchema`, 20 values). It was `z.array(z.string())` and
  nothing reads it yet, but 38 packs free-typing flavour strings would have handed whoever
  implements hazards about 40 one-off synonyms to triage. The 28 new packs introduced only five new
  values (`cattle-crossing`, `rain-slick`, `river-fog`, `heat-shimmer`, `crowd-surge`); everything
  else reuses what Lagos and the C27 batch already established. `dummy.json`'s `"dust"` became
  `dust-plume`.

### A bug the packs exposed
Making 28 states playable meant playing a non-Lagos state for the first time, and the first thing
on screen was **"WELCOME TO LAGOS"** over the border gate — in Enugu, in Ogun, everywhere. The
finish line had the same bug with a Yoruba thank-you. Both strings were literals inside
`GameRenderer`, which nothing had ever caught because Lagos was the only playable state.

The copy now comes from the pack via two pure helpers in `trail.ts` (`gateSign`, `finishSign`), so
it is testable without WebGL. `signTexture` also gained a shrink-to-fit loop for the sub-line: it
was a fixed size, which was safe only while it was one hard-coded string, and state welcome lines
run from 15 to 40 characters. `RenderOpts.gate`/`.finish` are required, not optional, so a new
caller has to decide rather than silently inherit Lagos.

## Open
- **Landmarks are `verified: false`, and that flag is honest.** These are written from general
  knowledge, not checked against a source. Names, spellings and which city a landmark sits in all
  need a pass by someone who knows the state — especially the ones I was least sure of: Gombe's
  `Tudun Wada Square`, Zamfara's `Damba Square`, Kebbi's `Old City Gate` and Yobe's
  `Gashua Dune Road` are plausible rather than confirmed.
- **The non-Yoruba/Igbo/Hausa greetings need a native speaker.** Edo's `Obokhian!` is the one I am
  least able to check. Nine states have no farewell line at all, which is a deliberate gap, not an
  oversight — authoring one needs someone who speaks the language.
- **Every state still renders the Lagos world.** `palette` and `biomeKit` are carried by all 37
  packs and read by nothing; the BACKLOG item to wire them into `world.ts` is now worth doing,
  because there is finally content behind every kit. Sokoto looks exactly like Lagos today.
- Hazards are still flavour. Nothing in the sim or renderer reads the field.
- Browser-pane only: Enugu and Ogun were played start and finish; the other 26 were verified by
  loading every pack at runtime and by the test suite, not by playing them.
