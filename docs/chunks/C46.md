# C46 — the run is driven with a thumb
Status: done      Depends on: C22, C28, C44, C45
## Goal
Make the run playable one-handed on a phone. Five pads ran along the bottom of the screen and the
two that mattered most — brake and gas — were the two a thumb could not hold while also swiping for
a lane. Replace the pads with gestures, keep on screen only the two controls that are timed against
something the player has to see, and say what the beat is for.
## Done when
Lanes, hop, brake, horn and the beat are all reachable with one thumb, the brake can be held through
a full load without letting go of lane control, the keyboard still plays the whole game, and every
threshold a gesture is judged against lives in content.

## What was wrong

**The brake and the gas were the same thumb.** The brake is not a tap: a pickup wants
`zones.loadTicks` — a full second — of braking inside the zone, and the gas is held for the rest of
the run. Both were pads at the bottom of the screen, and lane changes were swipes across the middle
of it. One thumb cannot hold the bottom-left pad and swipe the middle at the same time, so the
phone build quietly asked for two hands on a game whose whole premise is a commute.

**Five pads covered the road.** Brake, horn, beat, perk and gas ran the full width at the bottom of
a 390-px screen, over the lane the player is driving in.

**The beat did not say what it was.** `BEAT` was an unlabelled pad that did nothing for most of a
run — the sim ignores `Btn.Tap` unless a venue window is open — and nothing on screen said the
window was open, how many taps it wanted, or that it was worth ×1.5 the crowd for that delivery. It
was the largest single bonus in the game behind an unmarked button.

**Two flicks inside one frame became one.** The old controller ORed its one-shot bits together and
cleared them after a tick. The sim is edge-triggered, so a second flick arriving in the same frame
as the first — ordinary when dodging a double spawn — was swallowed, and so was the second of two
flicks in consecutive ticks, because the bit never fell to zero between them.

**The swipe buzz ignored the player's haptics setting.** `navigator.vibrate(8)` sat in the pointer
handler with nothing checking the Settings toggle the player had already turned off.

## Shipped

**A pure gesture reader.** `apps/game/src/input/gestures.ts` holds the whole state machine — no DOM,
no timers, nothing but pointer positions and a wall-clock timestamp in, gestures out. That is what
makes a thumb-driven control scheme testable at all: the ten gesture tests run in the node
environment with no browser.

- **Flick left / right** — change lane.
- **Flick up** — hop.
- **Flick down** — brake, and it stays on for as long as the thumb stays on the glass. This is the
  one that unlocks the rest: the finger that is braking can keep flicking sideways for lanes, so
  loading at a pickup no longer costs lane control. A flick down and straight off the glass keeps
  braking for `brakeGraceMs` — enough to shed speed for a dodge, deliberately far short of
  `zones.loadTicks`, so a flick can never load a zone by itself.
- **Double tap** — the beat. One tap is one beat tap and the sim wants `bonuses.onBeatTaps` of them,
  which is now 2, so one double tap earns the bonus. The sim is still counting real taps; nothing
  credits a gesture with taps the player did not make.
- **Hold still** — the horn. It fires once per press and is not also counted as a tap.

**The convoy drives itself.** `controls.autoGas` removes the gas pedal and leaves the brake as the
only speed input. Gas beats brake inside the sim, so auto-gas stands down whenever the brake is on,
which a test holds.

**Two pads left, both of them clocks.** `BEAT` shows `double-tap · ×1.5` when idle and
`n/2 taps` with a draining bar while a venue window is open; `PERK` shows the Contender's perk by
name with its charge. Both are things the player is timing against something, which is the reason
they are still on screen and the brake is not. A `BRAKING` tell replaces the pad that used to light
up. A one-line gesture reminder fades itself out over `controls.hintMs` with a CSS animation rather
than a timer, so a HUD re-render cannot restart it mid-run.

**One-shots queue, with a blank tick behind each.** Two flicks in one frame now reach the sim as two
rising edges three ticks apart instead of one.

**Everything a gesture is judged against is content.** `packages/content/data/controls.json`:
`swipeMinPx`, `swipeMaxMs`, `tapMaxMovePx`, `tapMaxMs`, `holdHornMs`, `brakeGraceMs`, `hintMs`,
`autoGas` and five haptic lengths. Content tests hold the invariants that matter — a flick must
clear the circle a tap may wander in, a hold must outlast the longest tap, the on-beat window must
be long enough to double-tap in, and the brake's run-on must be shorter than a load.

**The keyboard still plays the whole game.** `key(down, k)` is public for the same reason the
pointer entry points are, and a test drives the desktop controls through it.

**Haptics obey the setting**, with a different length per gesture.

## Also fixed in passing

- A held finger is the horn now, so the game surface turns off the platform's long-press callout,
  text selection and context menu, and the page turns off overscroll — a flick down on a phone was
  otherwise a pull-to-refresh.
- Tabbing away mid-run left the brake and horn stuck on; `blur` now clears them.
- The `BRAKING` tell is `#ffa05c`, not `--cr-jollof`: the brand orange measures 2.9:1 against the
  tell's own panel blended over white lane paint, which is the worst ground it sits on.

## Verified

- `pnpm verify` green: 305 tests (was 287) and 74 (was 69), lint and typecheck clean, size-check OK.
- `pnpm test:e2e` 5 passed, 1 skipped.
- Chrome at 390×844 and 1280×800: no horizontal overflow, pads 12 px off the bottom edge, the hint
  clears them by 12 px and is fully faded by 6 s, and the six new text rows measure 5.8:1 to 9.7:1
  against their own panels blended over white.
- A full Lagos leg driven through the real UI: flick down and hold loaded 8/8 at the pickup, the
  venue prompt appeared, the pad counted `1/2 taps` mid-double-tap, and `ON BEAT` fired.

## Open

- The gestures are tuned by driving them in Chrome with a mouse. Nothing here has been played with
  an actual thumb — the real-device playtest every track still owes.
- No left-handed or reduced-gesture mode: a player who cannot flick has the keyboard, which is not
  an answer on a phone.
- `controls.autoGas` is a flag with no UI. A player who wants manual gas can only get it by editing
  content.
- The tutorial card is still a wall of text shown once, not a guided first leg.
