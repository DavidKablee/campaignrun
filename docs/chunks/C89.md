# C89 — Contender avatars in Mobilise (opt-in)

Players asked for the person they see in Mobilise to be their Contender. That is the player's identity, so it stays private unless they choose otherwise.

## What changed
- **The question**: the first Mobilise visit with a Contender picked shows "Show your Contender here?" with "Keep me anonymous" and "Yes, show {name}". It says plainly that the room will see it. Asked once.
- **Account**: a Mobilise section with a toggle "Show my Contender to people in my room".
- **Server**: `users.mobilise_contender_id` and `mobilise_asked_at`. `GET/POST /api/places/identity` (`{contenderId | null}`, validated against the Contender list, 20 per minute). `PublicMember.look` is `{contenderId} | null` and replaces the old unused `color`. The join call no longer accepts a look or colour from the client. Changing the choice tells the room at once (`hub.setLook`).
- **Look**: an opted-in avatar is built with `getCharacter` plus a sash in the party colour. A plain citizen is the seeded person as before.
- Not tied to `contender_consent_at` (map colouring): different purpose, different toggle.

## Rules kept
Guardrail 3 (no logos, symbols, slogans, catchphrases, real-person mapping), guardrail 4 (locals never in party colours), guardrail 10 (Contender private by default). GUARDRAILS 13 updated.
