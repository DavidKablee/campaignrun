# C62 — Ogun's world

## What
- **Landmarks** (all `verified: false`): Kemta-Itoku Adire Market, Ogun River Bridges, Interchange Park (generic; the Sagamu interchange had tanker fires in 2019 and 2025), Ibara Flyover and Footbridge, Township Stadium (generic; the Ilaro stadium rested on one weak source). Olumo Rock is not a venue: it is a living shrine site, so Ogun's skyline carries only unmarked grey granite domes. Palette sky `#B4C8D2`, fog `#E3E0D3`, ground `#9A8E7C`, accent adire indigo `#2B3045`. `signatureRide` danfo → keke. Welcome "Ẹ káàbọ̀ sí Ògùn!"; the farewell now has the tone marks ("Ẹ ṣeun, Ògùn!").
- **Route:** adire quarter → river bank → gateway interchange (silos, tippers) → Ibara flyover (contemporary) → Ilaro timber road → Ofada farm belt (rice, rubber) → quarry and cement road → civic ridge (contemporary). Library `forest-hills.json`.
- **Look:** `ogun-slab` road (pale concrete, granite-chip shoulders), `ogun-block` facade, `ogun` shop boards, `yoruba-egba` dress (indigo family only; no crowd in one print), `ogun-domes` skyline.
- **New styles:** lots `adire-yard`, `boulder-house`, `silo-works`; flora `rubber`, `kola`, `granitedome`; kerbside `blockyard`, `tipper`, `kolabaskets`, `dyetubs`.
- **Set pieces** (`render/world/states/ogun.ts`): `ogun.adire-market`, `ogun.river-bridges` (an old truss and a new beam bridge), `ogun.interchange-park` (a plain tapering shaft, no arms, disc or emblem), `ogun.flyover` (with a covered footbridge), `ogun.stadium`.
