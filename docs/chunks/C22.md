# C22 — Animation & movement feel
Status: done      Depends on: C21
## Goal
Fix the "robotic" movement feel: the old `stand`/`run` pose was a hard boolean switch that snapped instantly at a speed threshold, hop was a binary tuck override, and the camera hard-set its position every frame with no lag. Sim numbers (speed/momentum/dents) are untouched — only how they're animated/rendered changed.
## Done when
Movement reads as weighty, not lockstep, on a real run; frame-time budget holds; `pnpm verify` green.
## Shipped
- `character.ts` `poseCharacter('run', …)`: now continuous in `speed01` via a smoothstep `amt` — stride length/frequency/arm swing all scale with it, and idle breathing fades in as `amt→0` instead of discontinuously switching to a separate `stand` pose. Secondary motion: torso/head trail the leg phase by ~0.055s (overlap).
- `GameRenderer.ts`:
  - Gameplay always calls `poseCharacter(rig, 'sit'|'run', t, speed01)` — the old `moving ? 'run' : 'stand'` boolean (and the `0.5 + speed01` hack that went with it) is gone.
  - Hop tuck now blends in/out via `hopAmt = min(1, hop/1.1)` lerped against whatever the run pose already set, instead of a hard overwrite past `hop > 0.05`.
  - Landing squash-stretch: `prevHop`/`squashT` detect the hop→ground frame and scale `rig.body` (flatten then ease back over 0.16s) — plus a matching small camera-Y dip.
  - Camera position/FOV are eased toward target each frame (`camX/camY/camZ` lerp at `dt*9`, FOV at `dt*7`) instead of hard-set — gives the chase cam inertia instead of being glued to the player.
  - Added a small camera roll (`camLean`, eased) on lane changes, applied via `camera.rotateZ()` after `lookAt`.
  - Hit shake switched from per-frame `Math.random()` jitter to a decaying `sin`/`cos` oscillation — reads as an impact rattle instead of static noise.
  - New speed-streak juice (`spawnStreak`/`this.streaks`) above `speed01 > 0.62`, off under reduce-motion/data-saver.
  - Fixed a real pre-existing bug found while in this code: the okada seat offset (`buildPlayer`) still hardcoded the **old** `HIP_Y = 0.94` from the pre-C21 skeleton; the chibi skeleton's `HIP_Y` is `0.5`. Left uncorrected it would have seated the rider at the wrong height on every okada run.
## Open (explicitly deferred, not silently dropped)
- Dust/exhaust particles and lane-change tire-skid decals — natural fits for C23 once the world rebuild adds real ground-decal infrastructure; skipped here to keep this chunk's diff focused on poseCharacter/camera.
- `cheer`/`wave` poses (result screen, studio, showcase) were left as discrete snaps — they aren't blended against anything else during gameplay, so the "robotic" problem doesn't apply to them the way it did to stand/run.
