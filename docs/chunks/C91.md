# C91 — The Campaign Phone, daily jobs and a streak

The reason to come back tomorrow, and a menu that is one thing instead of a scatter of trays.

## What changed
- **Campaign Phone** (header button): a handset sheet with a home screen of app tiles. Apps: **Jobs**, **Friends**, **Rooms** (the C90 screens, moved in), **Messages** (opens the phrase tray), **Board** (Most active states today, from `GET /api/places/activity`), **Convoy** (your ride, Following, Funds, next unlock) and **Settings** (language, your look in places, muted players with Unmute). A new app is one entry in `PhoneSheet.tsx`. Nothing on it can be typed into.
- **Campaign Jobs**: three a day, the same for everyone on a Lagos day, chosen from the date alone (`jobsForDay`). The pool is in `places.json` (canvass, flyers, rally, wave, say, visit Naija Square, add a friend). A day never has more than one job that needs another player, so a solo player can always finish all three. Progress is counted on the server from accepted actions (`api/src/placeJobs.ts`); there is no route a client can report progress on.
- **Pay**: a finished job pays a little coins and Following; all three pay a bonus once. All of it goes through `grant`, which applies the same daily coin and Following caps as any action, so jobs never widen them.
- **Streak**: consecutive Lagos days with at least one job done. Miss a day and it reads as nothing until the next job (the best stays). Milestones (3, 7, 14, 30 days) earn a title and a small bonus, once each.
- **Titles**: Market Regular, Park Veteran, Stadium Faithful, Town Crier. Chosen from a fixed list, worn from Jobs, shown under the name on your tag (`PublicMember.title`). Nobody types one.
- **Jobs strip**: the dock shows `Jobs 1/3 · 2-day streak` and opens the phone's Jobs.
- Job payouts count toward today's totals on the bars.
