# C58 — Borno's world

## Why
Same as C55; dossier `docs/research/states/borno.md`. Borno is drawn as a generic Kanuri-sahel town with role-named venues and no real place names beyond the river, because the dossier flagged conflict-linked imagery.

## What
- **Landmarks** (`data/states/borno.json`, all `verified: false`): Cap & Cloth Market, Flyover Road, City Gate, Main Stadium, Ngadda Riverbank (replacing Monday Market, State Museum, El-Kanemi Stadium, Sanda Kyarimi Park, Ramat Polytechnic). Palette sky `#DCD3BD`, fog `#ECE3CD`, ground `#C9B38C`, accent `#8B5A2B`; welcome and farewell kept.
- **`signatureRide` okada → keke** (sim data, regenerated with `pnpm content:roads`): okada are banned in the city and the dossier called them a poor fit. Rivers still says danfo against the dossier's keke; left alone.
- **Route:** market lane → flyover road → city gate → stadium road → riverbank → neem avenue → farm edge (masakwa sorghum) → city centre. Library `data/world/sahel-savanna.json` (shared with Kano and Kaduna).
- **Look:** `sahel-sand` road, `sand-clay` facade, `borno` shop boards (English trade words only), `kanuri-north` dress (creams, sand, indigo, no solid red/green/blue caps), `chad-plain` skyline. Keke are mustard `#D9A22B` with a black roof, just over the party-yellow distance limit.
- **New styles:** lots `kanuri-compound`, `lockup-row`; wall `clay-low`; flora `reedbed`; crop `masakwa`; kerbside `waterbags`, `caprack`, `clothbolts`, `matscreen`, `donkeycart`, `kekerank`.
- **Set pieces** (`render/world/states/borno.ts`): `borno.cap-market`, `borno.flyover`, `borno.city-gate` (a monument beside the road, no barrier, post or uniform), `borno.stadium`, `borno.riverbank`.
- **Left out on purpose:** mosques, checkpoints, smoke, uniforms, tents, flood, motorcycles, camels.
