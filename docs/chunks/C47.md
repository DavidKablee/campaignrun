# C47 — screens that fit a phone
Status: done      Depends on: C38, C40, C44, C45, C46
## Goal
Stop the menu and pre-run screens from being documents. Measure every screen at phone size and get
the ones you act on down to about one screen, with the button you came for visible without
scrolling.
## Done when
No screen in the normal flow runs past roughly one and a quarter phone screens, the primary action
on each is above the fold at load, and nothing regresses on a desktop width.

## What was wrong

Measured in Chrome at 390×844, before this chunk:

| screen | height | |
|---|---|---|
| Convoy Yard | 3125 px | **3.70×** the viewport |
| briefing | 1552 px | 1.84× |
| campaign map | 1458 px | 1.73× |
| roster | 1402 px | 1.66× |
| Account | 1329 px | 1.57× |
| About | 1190 px | 1.41× |

**The Yard was three and a half screens of stacked sheets** — goals, then every upgrade, then a
showroom card per ride, each card carrying the five handling bars C44 added. Comparing two rides
meant scrolling until they were too far apart to compare.

**The briefing put Start the run below the fold.** The route trail was seven full-width cards
(nearly half a screen for a list of place names) and the ride picker was seven cards two-up, four
rows deep. Both grew in C44 and C45 without anyone measuring the result.

**The roster hid the roster.** The hero portrait is a 5:4 half-body render — a third of the
viewport on its own — and the six Contender cards sat under it, so the thing you were choosing from
was entirely below the fold.

## Shipped

**The Yard is tabbed: Goals · Upgrades · Rides.** One panel at a time, each about one screen
(1.02× / 1.21× / 1.21×). `Tabs` and `TabPanel` live in `MenuChrome.tsx` next to `Sheet`, using the
same pill styling as the auth screen's tabs, with real `tablist`/`tab`/`tabpanel` roles. The strip
sticks to the top of the scrollport and carries a band of the screen's own ground, so panel text
cannot slide visibly between the pills.

**Two long card lists became swipe strips.** The showroom (`.cr-ride-grid`) and the briefing's ride
picker (`.cr-rides`) scroll sideways on a phone with scroll snapping, and revert to the original
grid at 700 px and up, where there is room for it. The showroom's cards line up, so the same stat
row sits at the same height on every ride — which is the comparison the stacked layout made
impossible.

**The briefing's route trail is wrapped pills** instead of a two-column card grid: the same seven
names in three lines. Hazards are two-up — their column floor dropped from 160 px to 145 px because
the brief panel's inner width on a 390-px phone is 318, so at 160 the second column never fit and
three hazards were always three rows.

**Board and Start the run are pinned to the bottom of the briefing.** Whatever is left to scroll,
the two things you came to that screen to do stay on screen. Sticky, so on a desktop — where the
screen fits — the row simply sits where it always did.

**The roster is a hero and a strip.** The six cards scroll sideways directly under the hero, and
the portrait is capped at `clamp(190px, 27vh, 260px)` on a phone. The commit button is visible at
load on both 390×844 and 360×740.

**The map's two long lists are capped** at 36 vh (the 37 state chips) and 34 vh (the hex stage) on a
phone. The hex stage is `aria-hidden`, so the chip list is the only way to pick a state without a
pointer — it stays complete and keyboard-reachable, it just scrolls in its own box.

## After

| briefing | 1.84× | **1.14×** | Start visible at load |
| roster | 1.66× | **1.11×** | commit button visible at load |
| campaign map | 1.73× | 1.71× | caps only bite on shorter phones |
| summary | — | 1.14× | |
| splash, age gate, studio, Settings | — | 1.00× | already fitted |

Desktop (1280×800) after: roster 1.07×, briefing 1.15×, Yard goals 1.00×. No horizontal overflow at
either width, and no console errors on any screen.

## Not changed, and why

**About (1.41×) and Account (1.57×) keep their length.** Both are mostly prose, and the prose is the
disclosure, the privacy notice and the consent explanations. Shortening those to win a screen of
scroll trades a legal and ethical obligation for a layout preference. A page of text that a reader
scrolls is working as intended; a game screen that hides its own button is not.

**The map stays at 1.7×.** Its length is its content: a ticker, four stats, the party tally, the
board, the state list and a focus panel whose every line is a fact about the state you picked.
Nothing in it is padding.
