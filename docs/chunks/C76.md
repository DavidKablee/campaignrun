# C76 — Cross River's world

## What
- **Landmarks** (all `verified: false`): Central Market (lock-up rows, plantain and fish tables; generic, so Watt and Marian with their fire histories are not named), River Quay (a plain concrete quay with a harbour crane and mangroves; **no resort, museum or boat**, because the Marina Resort stands on a former slave port and closed after a 2023 boat accident), Carnival Road (a wide avenue with an **empty** grandstand and muted pennant lines; no crowd, no bikers, because of the December 2022 tragedy), Trade Hall (a plain exhibition hall and gate with a pond; no disaster, Tinapa is not named) and Township Stadium (generic). Palette ground `#2F5D3A` (the draft repeated five states), accent deep plum `#7A2E4A` (the draft `#E0319A` was 34 from party magenta). Market tomato colours were moved to `#b0823a` and `#a04a3a` to stay away from party orange. `signatureRide` **danfo → keke** (okada has been banned in Calabar since 2009, pool taxis and keke are the main transport; road table regenerated). Hazards crowd-surge, rain-slick, go-slow. Welcome "Welcome to Calabar — carnival city!" kept; farewell "Thank you, Cross River!" added (English only; an Efik line needs a native speaker).
- **Route:** market → quay road → carnival road → hall road → stadium road → raffia belt → tailors' lane → plaza street (finish). Library `creek-delta.json` (new `cross-river.*` districts).
