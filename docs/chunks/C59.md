## Why
Third batch (plan: `docs/research/batch-3-plan.md`); dossier `docs/research/states/enugu.md`.

## What
- **Landmarks** (`data/states/enugu.json`, all `verified: false`): Hillside Market (generic, the dossier's New Haven Market had one weak source), Central Station Sky Bridge, Township Stadium (generic; the real ground is person-named), Nike Lake, Udi Hills Lookout (the rock-cut hairpin road; the draft's "Milken Hill" is person-named). Palette sky `#9FBACB`, fog `#CCD6CE`, ground `#7A4A2E`, accent coal charcoal `#34383B`; welcome "Nnọọ, Enugu!" (the "Coal City" suffix is dropped), farewell kept. `signatureRide` stays keke.
- **Route:** Ogui market lanes (stepped hillside terraces) → Central Station → stadium road → Nike Lake road → Udi hills road (pines, chevrons, cut bank) → workshop hillside → Ngwo farm belt → city centre (slab tower, plaza blocks, median). Library `data/world/forest-hills.json` (shared with Oyo).
- **Look:** `enugu-hill` road (the Oyo asphalt with an iron-brown shoulder), `plaster` facade, `enugu` shop boards, `igbo-enugu` dress, `udi-cuesta` skyline (three layered flat-topped ridges, no spire or dome).
- **New styles:** lots `hillside-terrace`, `gra-cottage`, `slab-tower`, `welding-shed`; flora `pine`, `eucalyptus`; kerbside `gatestack`, `busshelter`, `chevrons`, `minibus`.
- **Set pieces** (`render/world/states/enugu.ts`): `enugu.market`, `enugu.skybridge` (unlabelled, board reads "Central Station"), `enugu.stadium`, `enugu.lake`, `enugu.lookout`.
- **Shared helper:** `stadiumBlock` in `states/common.ts` (Enugu, Plateau and Ogun use it).
