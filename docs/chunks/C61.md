# C61 — Katsina's world

## What
- **Landmarks** (all `verified: false`): Grain Market (generic; the central market burned in 2021), Old City Wall and Gate (a red wall section; the draft's "Gobarau Minaret" is a mosque minaret and was dropped), Old Katsina College, GRA Flyover (unlabelled: the board names the road, not the project), Ajiwa Lakeside. Palette sky `#CFDBE0`, fog `#EFE0CC`, ground rose sand `#AB7F66`, accent `#A0564A` (off the orange the other Hausa states share). `signatureRide` okada → keke, drawn as cream with a brown roof so it differs from Kano's yellow. Welcome and farewell kept.
- **Route:** Kasuwa market quarter → old wall quarter → college quarter → GRA and interchange → Ajiwa road (irrigated plots, lake) → industrial edge → farm belt → city centre. Library `sahel-savanna.json`, with its own facade and road so it reads differently from Kano and Borno.
- **Look:** `katsina-rose` road, `relief-render` facade (cream and rose with a band of raised lozenges), `katsina` shop boards, `hausa-katsina` dress, `katsina-flat` skyline.
- **New styles:** lots `relief-compound` (low stepped parapet, no horns), `redbrick-hall`; flora `faidherbia`; crop `irrigation`; kerbside `watertub`, `kekecream`.
- **Set pieces** (`render/world/states/katsina.ts`): `katsina.grain-market`, `katsina.city-wall`, `katsina.college`, `katsina.interchange`, `katsina.ajiwa`.
- **Left out on purpose:** minaret, dome, palace, horsemen, regalia, herds, uniforms, school scenes.
