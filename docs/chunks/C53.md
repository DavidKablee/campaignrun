# C53 — Anambra's world

## Why
Anambra rendered the Lagos world with a green tint. See ADR-0010, C50–C52. The dossier is `docs/research/states/anambra.md`.

## What
- **Landmarks** (`data/states/anambra.json`, all `verified: false`): Onitsha Main Market (market), Second Niger Bridge (bridge), Onitsha River Port (waterfront), Nnewi City Centre Roundabout (square), Awka Township Stadium (stadium). The pack's "Bridgehead Crossing", "Ekwulobia Square", "Nkisi Park" and "Awka Civic Ground" could not be found in any source. Palette from the dossier (sky `#8FB4CE`, fog `#DCE6E6`). Welcome "Nnọọ! Anambra is open." and farewell "Daalụ, Anambra!" are kept as shipped (best supported in the dossier).
- **Route:** Onitsha market → Obosi bridgehead (toll plaza, deck, Niger on the left) → river port → Nnewi → Awka → Agulu/Nanka red-earth country (mansions, cashew) → Ekwulobia → Omor floodplain (rice, yam barns, raffia); start at an Obosi junction (red-earth shoulder, keke rank, mansion and zinc bungalows). Library `data/world/farmland-river.json`.
- **Look:** `laterite-wash` road (dark patched asphalt, red laterite washed in from the edges), `plaza` facades (painted render, balcony rails), `igbo` shop boards (English trade words and "Mama Put", nothing personal or religious), `igbo-southeast` dress (indigo, wine, ochre, teal; no caps, no red cap, one isiagu in five tops, single colour), `niger-bluffs` skyline (far bank hills, faint bridge, cranes), billboards of plain trade words.
- **New styles:** lots `market-block`, `mansion`, `zinc-bungalow`, `factory-shed`, `port-shed`; flora `cashew`, `raffia`, `yambarn`; crop `rice`; kerbside `tyres`, `keke`, `cloth`.
- **Set pieces** (`render/world/states/anambra.ts`): `anambra.main-market`, `anambra.toll-plaza` (+ `anambra.toll-clear`), `anambra.bridge-deck`, `anambra.river-port`, `anambra.nnewi-roundabout`, `anambra.awka-stadium`.
- Rivers' water default recoloured grey-green and its lighthouse moved closer (review fixes, same chunk of work).

## Not done
- Igbo welcome/farewell and any shop words need a native read. Onitsha dialect differs.
- Shapes are inferred: market block heights, toll canopy, the roundabout's centre (unknown in sources), stadium form, mansion style.
- The old Niger Bridge (steel truss) is not modelled; it would duplicate Delta's.
- Agulu–Nanka gully is not shown (ongoing landslides); no sacred sites.
- Signature ride is already keke; no hazard visuals; no real-phone test.
