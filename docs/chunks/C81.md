# C81 — FCT's world, the Final Week, the Weekly Tour, and a live C14 check

From the C13–C19 review (`docs/research/c13-c19-review.md`). Built in the order that review gave.

## What

### FCT (the finale state)
- **World:** library `guinea-savanna` (new `fct.*` districts), road `fct-asphalt`, facade `fct-render`, shop set `fct`, dress `abuja-city`, skyline `fct-capital`. Eight districts: market zone, boulevard, lake road, park avenue, stadium road, estate road, satellite town, city centre (finish).
- **Five landmarks** (all `verified: false`): Wuse Market (a concrete market hall with produce and cloth stalls), Boulevard Roundabout (a landscaped roundabout with a fountain basin), Jabi Lake Waterfront (promenade, jetty, pedal boats), Millennium Park (stepped amphitheatre, fountain pool), City Stadium (generic). Pieces are in `render/world/states/fct.ts`.
- **Left out on purpose:** every government and electoral building, the national stadium (person-named), places of worship, the rock beside the presidential complex, flags (the old "boulevard flagpoles" scenery line is gone). Okada and keke stay rare at the kerb because they are restricted in the central districts.
- **Evidence:** `docs/research/states/fct.md`, the thinnest dossier in the set: two search passes and general knowledge, every claim single-source.
- **Checks:** the state tour passes the draw and triangle budget at every place (the first run failed at 159 draws because the preview server held an old build; it passes on a fresh build); the balance bot holds Contender parity (±3%) on the FCT hazards.

### Final Week and the close
- `calendar.finalWeekFrom` is now the Saturday 00:00 Lagos time before the close (it was a Monday that nothing read). The Final Week moves with the close date if `flags.json` moves it.
- During the Final Week the finale state is featured, and the Weekly Tour bonus can be earned once a day instead of once a week. After the close the game is in practice mode (C78).

### Weekly Tour, Final Week and Rally Saturday banners
- `liveEvents.ts`: one open state outside Lagos is featured per Lagos-time week (chosen from the states open at Monday 06:00, so a batch unlocking on Monday does not change it); the first finished rally there pays ₦200 (`live-events.json`). In the Final Week it is FCT and ₦150 a day.
- Only coins change: the sim, boards and replays are untouched. Quick rallies and breakdowns do not pay it.
- Banners on the splash, a note on the result screen, and a line on the focused state on the board.
- `flags.json` may carry `"featured": "<state id>"` to pick the state by hand (ignored if not open or unknown).
- **Rally Saturday** already existed as the sim's double Crowd between 6 and 9 pm Lagos time on Saturdays (C30), but nothing told the player. It is now announced all Saturday ("Tonight, 6 to 9 pm") and while it is on. The first draft of this chunk added a second, payout lift on top of it; that was removed as double counting.
- Store version 12 (`tourClaimedKey`).

### C14 against a live API
- Ran the API locally on Postgres, posted verified runs (real replays) from three consenting accounts in Kano and Rivers, confirmed the standings route publishes them above the floor and that the Board shows "Live · 2" with the right leaders. Standings count only verified email addresses, as designed, so the seeded dev accounts had to be verified first.
- The local floor is `CR_MIN_PLAYERS=1` from `.env`; the shipped floor stays the content value.
- Not built, on purpose: other players' Contenders on the map.

### C16
Already closed: the per-ride balance bot (C26) and the hardest-roads check (C45) exist, and rivals are identical for every Contender (`integrity.test.ts`). The review's claim that a per-ride pass was missing was wrong; FCT's hazards were added to the hardest-roads list.
