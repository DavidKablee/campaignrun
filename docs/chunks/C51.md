# C51 — Kano's world

## Why
Kano rendered the Lagos world with a brown tint. See ADR-0010 and C50. The dossier is `docs/research/states/kano.md`.

## What
- **Landmarks** (`data/states/kano.json`, all `verified: false`): Kurmi Market (market), Kofar Mata Dye Pits (landmark), Dala Hill (landmark), Kano Race Course (stadium), Zoo Road Trade Fair Complex (landmark). The pack's "Challawa Bridge" and "Sabon Gari Square" were not found in any source; the zoo and the Sabon Gari market carry a person's name. Palette moved to the dossier's haze (sky `#E7CE9C`, fog `#EFDDBB`, ground `#B8895A`).
- **Route** (one district per segment): Kurmi → Kofar Mata → Dala and the walls → Nassarawa/Fagge → Zoo Road (the contemporary district, 3–5 storey plazas) → Sabon Gari → Dawanau → the walls; start in a millet-field compound lane; finish at the walls. Library: `data/world/sahel-savanna.json`.
- **Set pieces** (`render/world/states/kano.ts`): `kano.market-gate`, `kano.dye-pits`, `kano.city-gate`, `kano.dala-hill`, `kano.flyover`, `kano.race-course`, `kano.trade-fair`. Venue pieces stand on the left (the rally stage is on the right); a piece owns its side of its chunk, so the plots, walls, stalls and trees that would stand in it are left out.
- **Look:** `sahel-dust` road, `mud` and `block` facades, `sahel` shop boards (ochre, indigo, sand), the `hausa-north` dress set (riga, kaftan, kube, turban, hijab, wrapper), a laterite Dala skyline, ambient dust, generic billboards (no names, no religious phrases).
