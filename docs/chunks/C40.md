# C40 — Pick your Contender, and the page after a run
Status: done      Depends on: C38, C39
## Goal
The two screens on either side of a run — the roster you arrive through and the result you leave
through — were the last pre-C38 pages a player meets before the briefing and HUD. Bring them into
the same language as C38/C39, and fix what the redesign exposed.
## Done when
`pnpm verify` green; both screens scroll and read at 390×844 and 1280×800; no behaviour change ships
without a test.

## Shipped
`App.tsx` drops from 1223 to 879 lines: `Roster` and `Summary` moved to `ui/RosterScreen.tsx` and
`ui/SummaryScreen.tsx`, next to the C38/C39 screens. `usePortraits` went with the roster (its only
caller); `useStatePack` and `daysLeft` became `statePack.ts` and `campaignClock.ts`, since Briefing
and the run still need them.

### Pick your Contender
- One white floating hero card on the dark ground: portrait over the Contender's own palette, then
  archetype, name, Party, catchphrase, and the perk and trade-off as two **named** rows. They used
  to be two yellow pills that said what each did but not which one was the cost.
- Six white cards with a green selection ring, a `Yours` chip on your own Contender and a `Locked`
  chip on one you can no longer take.
- `copy.rosterFair` says out loud that the six are balanced (guardrail 5) and `copy.rosterLock` says
  what the lock costs, instead of leaving both to a one-line hint.

**Selecting and committing are now separate.** The hero only changed on `mouseEnter`/`focus`, and a
tap went straight to the studio — so on a phone, where there is no hover, the perk, trade-off and
Party of the other five were unreachable: you chose a character from a name and a thumbnail. Now a
tap previews and nothing reaches the store; the hero's button is the only thing that writes a
choice. Hover-preview is gone entirely rather than made conditional, because a card sliding under a
still cursor when the hero resizes re-selected it behind your back (seen in the Playwright run).

That split also closes two defects in the lock:
- Tapping a Contender you can no longer switch to used to `return` silently. The card looked exactly
  like the five that worked. It now previews like any other and the hero says why it cannot be taken.
- The one free post-lock switch was spent on the first tap, with no confirmation. `lockAfterFinish`
  never clears, so that tap was the last word on a campaign-long choice. It now asks first.

`rosterCommit()` in `src/roster.ts` is the pure core of that (`open` / `confirm-switch` / `blocked`),
tested in `test/roster.test.ts` — the game's Vitest runs in `node`, so testable behaviour has to
leave the component.

### The page after a run
- One result card: outcome and state as an eyebrow, the tier as the heading (green, or ink on a
  breakdown), the Crowd big, then who ran it and on what.
- The four figures move to the C39 white stat cards, 2×2 on a phone and 4-up from 560px.
- Posting and sharing each became a sheet that says what it does *before* you press it: that the
  local board is this device, that your board name is public on any board you post to, and whether
  your Contender will be named on the share card. A breakdown now says why it cannot post, instead
  of silently omitting the field.

**It could not scroll.** `.cr-screen--summary` centred its children with `justify-content: center`
and no `overflow` (the same rule the C39 settings family was fixed out of). The result page is 962px
tall at 390×844, so ~120px of it — the board field and the navigation — had nowhere to go.

**The board-name field was invisible.** `.cr-board-submit input` set `color: var(--cr-ink)` (#0b1220)
on `background: rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.55)`: near-black on near-black. Whatever you typed, you could not
read. It now uses the C38 `.cr-field` control, measured at `rgb(22,32,43)` on `rgb(255,255,255)`.

### Found on the way
`.cr-daily-chip` set `color: var(--cr-ink)` as its body colour while living on dark screens, so the
challenge *label* — the only part that says what today's challenge is — was dark-on-dark on the
briefing and after a run. Only the "Daily" tag and the naira figure, which are `--cr-danfo`, were
ever readable. The chip's base colour is now `--cr-paper`; the C39 override keeps it inked inside
the Yard's white sheet.

`CLOSE_UTC` was a `Date.UTC(2027, 0, 15, 22, 59)` literal in `App.tsx` beside `calendar.closeAt`
holding the same moment in content — two copies of the date the player is counting down to.
`campaignClock.ts` reads the calendar. The roster's "Lagos" pill was also hard-coded while you may
be campaigning anywhere; it reads `activeStateId` now.
