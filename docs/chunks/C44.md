# C44 — seven rides, not one ride with more seats
Status: done      Depends on: C26, C39
## Goal
Make the ride ladder a set of choices instead of a price list. Every rung added seats and nothing
else, so the right ride was always the most expensive one you owned.
## Done when
Each ride handles measurably differently in the sim, each one costs the player something, the Yard
says what that cost is, and Contender parity still holds on all seven.

## What was wrong

**A ride was its seat count.** The sim knew four things about one: `size` (which gaps block it),
`ploughsLight`, `freeHitPerRun` and `magnetBonusMm` — all added in C26, three of them held by a
single ride each. Nothing else about a danfo differed from an okada. The flavour text promised
otherwise: the Danfo's own `trait` said "slow lane change" and the Okada's said "Fast", and neither
sentence had ever been true. So the ladder was monotonic — more Following bought more seats at no
cost, and nobody would ever pick a keke again once a danfo was in the yard. Six of the seven
assets were a tutorial for the seventh.

**The Grand Convoy was honest about being nothing.** Its trait read "Cosmetic flex; requires Spread
badge", and the badge it required does not exist (C26 recorded that). 120,000 Following bought a
different mesh.

**Momentum had never worked.** Found while wiring per-ride top speed: `deliverCrowd` multiplies by
`state.momentum`, and momentum only grows while `speed > maxSpeedUm × fastThresholdPermille`, with
that threshold set to **1050 permille** of a speed the sim clamps to 1000 permille of. The condition
is unsatisfiable by construction, so momentum sat at its floor (1000 = ×1.0) for every run ever
played, and the HUD showed a meter that could not move. Probed to be sure before changing it: trek,
keke and grand convoy all held gas for 600 ticks and ended at momentum 1000 with the bar at 349,650
and the hard ceiling at 333,000.

## Shipped

**Handling, as permille of the sim's own numbers.** Each ride carries a `handling` block — lane
change, pickup, top speed, braking, hop airtime — plus `hitDropPermille` and `rivalDrawPermille`.
Every field defaults to 1000, so a pack that omits the block drives exactly as it did before C44
(there is a test for that). The sim keeps one set of base values and a ride only says how it differs
from them; `step()` multiplies the ride in first and the Contender's trade-off second, in that fixed
order, because the ghost replay has to reproduce a run tick for tick.

What that buys, measured in `packages/sim/test/rides.test.ts`: a grand convoy takes longer to cross
a lane than an okada, each ride tops out at its own ceiling, a coaster is slower to pick up speed
than a keke, a convoy has to start braking earlier than a trek to reach load speed, a trek gets more
airtime out of a hop than a keke, and a knock scatters 35 of 100 carried on an okada against 17 on a
grand convoy.

**Every ride now costs something**, in a `tradeoff` field that sits beside `trait` — the upside and
what you pay for it, as two halves of the same sentence. The Okada is the fastest thing out and
loses 40% more of its crowd to a knock. The Grand Convoy holds the most, ploughs barriers and has
the widest magnet, and draws 60% more rival traffic than anything else on the road, which is the one
thing C26 left it needing.

**Momentum is reachable.** The threshold is now measured against *this ride's* ceiling rather than
the config's, which is both the fix and the only form of it that works: measured against the config,
the Trek's 82% ceiling would sit permanently under the bar, so slow rides would inherit the exact
cliff being removed. `fastThresholdPermille` is 950. All seven rides reach `momentum.max`, and the
C05/C26 balance bots still hold the six Contenders inside ±3% mean Crowd on every ride — checked
with the shipped handling applied, which it never was before: `runClean` spelled the ride fields out
by hand and had been silently testing a neutral version of all seven.

**The Yard became the showroom.** Each card states the upside, the cost, and a bar per handling stat
measured against the keke — worse to the left of a centre line, better to the right, so the shape of
a card reads before any of its words do. The keke shows no bars at all, which is the honest rendering
of "Balanced". The briefing (which is where you actually choose) gets the two sentences only; the
numbers belong in the Yard, two taps from the road is the wrong place for a table.

**One bundle for the sim.** `rideSimTraits(ride)` replaces three hand-written copies of the same
field list — the live run, the ghost re-simulation and the balance bot. They had already drifted.

## What this chunk found in its own work

- **The bar direction is not the sign of the number.** An okada scatters 40% *more* of its crowd, so
  "+40%" is bad news: direction follows the stat's tone, never its sign. A test pins both.
- **Written copy claimed the opposite of the code.** The first draft of the heavy rides' traits said
  "wades floods". C26's rule makes a flood transparent to S and M and blocks **L** — the heavy rides
  are the ones it stops. Prose cannot be tested, but the claims that map to a mechanic can be, and
  now are: a ride whose copy mentions floodwater must be size L, one that mentions a narrow gap must
  be S, one that mentions ploughing must actually plough.
- **4.49:1 is a fail.** The stat figures were drawn in `--cr-green` and `#b3261e` on the card's own
  tint, which measures 4.49 against AA's 4.5 for 11px bold. The bars keep those colours (a graphic
  needs 3:1); the figures are a shade darker. Every text style on the card now measures ≥ 4.85 —
  the contrast pass C43 said CI could not do, done by hand again.
- **The briefing is not the C43 trap**, though it looks like it: it scrolls 414px at 390×844 and the
  Start button is reachable. A flex item's automatic minimum size only applies while `overflow` is
  `visible`, and `.cr-screen--briefing` already sets `overflow: auto`. A `min-height: 0` was written
  and then removed, because shipping a no-op as a fix is worse than not shipping it. The comment
  explaining why stayed.

## Verified

`pnpm verify` green: **257 tests** (was 240 — 10 sim, 7 content), `pnpm lint` and `tsc --noEmit`
clean, e2e 5 passed / 1 skipped, `size-check: OK`. The Yard measured in Chrome at 390×844, 414×896
and 1280×800: no horizontal overflow at any width, every text style ≥ 4.85:1, bars and row counts
correct per ride. Clicked keke → okada → danfo → grand convoy in the briefing and the summary line
tracked the selection.

## Open

- **Nothing in the sim reads a state's `hazards`.** The obvious next step for this track, and the one
  that would make a ride choice depend on the road rather than on the yard: a hazard vocabulary has
  been closed since C34 and is still flavour data. "Which ride is right here" needs it.
- **No new vehicles.** The roster is the seven from C26; this chunk made them different rather than
  adding an eighth. A boat for the riverine states and a bicycle below the okada are the two that
  would carry their weight — both need a mesh.
- **Momentum now pays out**, so Crowd per run rises by up to 1.5× against every score recorded
  before this chunk. Local bests and the public boards hold pre-C44 numbers that were earned under a
  dead multiplier. `maxCrowdFor` already allowed for `momentum.max`, so nothing is rejected
  server-side, but the boards are not comparable across this line.
- A ride's `priceNgn` is still unused: rides unlock on Following, and nothing spends that number.
- The handling numbers are a first pass, tuned by reading the sim rather than by playing it on a
  phone. The real-device playtest owed by every track since C20 is where they get confirmed.
