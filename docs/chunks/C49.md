# C49 — Real traffic

## Why

Vehicles in the lanes were static props and the only moving traffic was a renderer-only pool on the shoulders. The player drove past parked things; nothing could be dodged, overtaken or reacted to. Danfo Run and Lagos Life both work the other way round.

## What

- **Sim.** `traffic` block in `sim.json` (zod in `@cr/content`). Entities gain `vz`, `xMm`, `mover`, `awake`, `passed`, `weaveTo`, `weaveState`, `weaveWait`. `updateTraffic` runs right after the player moves: it wakes a mover when the player is within `wakeMm`, advances it, runs the weave/walk state machines and emits `weaveWarn` / `closeCall`. `collide.ts` uses `xMm ?? laneCenterMm(lane)`. `course.ts` turns vehicle obstacles, a share of slots (`chancePermille`), rival convoys and the integrity cast into movers when a legal behaviour exists at that offset; every roll is drawn whether or not it is used, so a config change moves the course as little as possible.
- **Renderer.** `render/vehicles.ts` (builders, `animateVehicle`, near/far swap), `GameRenderer.syncEntities` (dormant movers not drawn, z interpolated by `vz`, x interpolated between sim ticks, walker bob and facing), `render/traffic.ts` deleted.
- **Feedback.** Toasts, `calloutForEvent` lines and two sounds for the new events.
- **Tests.** `packages/sim/test/traffic.test.ts`, a content test, and `test/helpers/dodgeBot.ts` (shared by the replay tests and the API replay harness).

## Open

- Never run on a phone. Draw calls are around 90–135 with traffic on screen in the desktop preview; the far LOD and Data Saver exist for that.
- Difficulty is measured only by a bot that looks ahead perfectly. Tune `traffic.chancePermille`, `weave.*` and `closeCallMm` after a human playtest.
- Density is bounded by the hazard gap (16–30 m): roughly half of a run's ~47 hazards are movers.
- A debug jump with `?debugZ=` skips the wake model, so passing vehicles can raise a `closeCall` radio line in the first second.
- Old ghosts and challenge links ran another road (`cr-ghosts-v2`).
