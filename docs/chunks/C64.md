# C64 — Bauchi's world

## What
- **Landmarks** (all `verified: false`): Grain & Produce Market (generic; the central market was bombed in 2014), Yankari Reserve Gate, Wikki Warm Spring, Old City Gate (generic; one of the nine real rebuilt gates, it replaces the unverified Railway Quarters Square) and Township Stadium (generic; the real stadium is person-named). Palette sky `#E2C99A`, fog `#F3E2C5`, ground `#A67C52`, accent `#8E6B3E` (the draft `#C45C26` is shared by five Hausa states). `signatureRide` hilux → keke (okada banned since 2020; the state handed out 345 keke). Hazards: dust-plume, cart-weave, go-slow (the draft cattle-crossing is dropped; it was herder-coded and no hazard visual exists yet). Welcome "Barka da zuwa Bauchi!", farewell "Na gode, Bauchi!".
- **Route:** produce market → reserve road (park gate) → spring and cliff road → old city gate quarter → stadium road → farm belt → old-town lane → city centre (finish). Library `guinea-savanna.json` (new `bauchi.*` districts).
- **Look:** `bauchi-sand` road (pale sand shoulders), `bauchi-render` facade, `bauchi` shop boards, `hausa-bauchi` dress (earth, indigo and white), `bauchi-hills` skyline (nine bare rock hills of different heights, one with a small tank, a pale roof band, a line of baobab and neem crowns).
- **Set pieces** (`render/world/states/bauchi.ts`): `bauchi.produce-market`, `bauchi.reserve-gate` (stacked sandstone piers, laterite track, no barrier arm), `bauchi.warm-spring` (pool under a stepped cliff, boardwalk, palms), `bauchi.old-gate` (arched passage in pale plaster with houses hemming in the stubs and a rock hill behind), `bauchi.stadium`.
- **Evidence:** `docs/research/states/bauchi.md` (second pass: nine hills, nine gates, walls, ride rule, palace, bombing, stadium cross-checked). Pack file: `packages/content/data/states/bauchi.json`; ride table regenerated with `pnpm content:roads`.
