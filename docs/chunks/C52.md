# C52 — Rivers' world

## Why
Rivers rendered the Lagos world with a green-grey tint. See ADR-0010, C50 and C51. The dossier is `docs/research/states/rivers.md`.

## What
- **Landmarks** (`data/states/rivers.json`, all `verified: false`): Mile One Market (market), Creek Road Waterfront (waterfront), Port Harcourt Pleasure Park (park), Garrison Flyover (bridge), Bonny River Lighthouse (landmark). Palette: sky `#93B4BB`, fog `#C6D3D0`, ground `#4F6B4B`, accent `#E09F3E`. Welcome "Una welcome to Garden City o!", farewell "Go well o! Make we see again."
- **Route** (one district per segment): Diobu market streets → Creek Road waterfront → Pleasure Park avenue → Garrison → Bonny estuary → Old G.R.A. → Trans-Amadi → Okrika creek village; start on Aba Road; finish in the creek village. Library: `data/world/creek-delta.json`.
- **Look:** `delta-wet` road (puddled, muddy shoulders), `plaster` and `plank` facades, `delta` shop boards, the `delta-south` dress set (no caps, wrappers, head-ties, teal/ochre/indigo/clay colours), an `oil-flares` skyline (tank farm, gantry cranes, one flare stack; skyline only), ambient none, generic billboards.
- **New ops and styles:** `waterEdge` (mud bank, grey-green water, canoes), kerbside `bole` and `basins`, flora `mango`, `oilpalm`, `nipa`, `mangrove`, `banana`, crop `cassava`, lot styles `stilt-house`, `colonial-bungalow`, `tank-farm`, `market-shed`. Ops take an optional `side` so a creek can run down one side only.
- **Set pieces** (`render/world/states/rivers.ts`): `rivers.mile-one`, `rivers.creek-jetty`, `rivers.pleasure-park`, `rivers.flyover` (a deck that crosses the road overhead, with `rivers.flyover-clear` owning the other side), `rivers.lighthouse`.
- Party-colour distance test now covers every dress colour and lot/bazaar palette.

## Not done
- Welcome and farewell Pidgin need a native read; shop and sign words too.
- Stilt-house proportions, keke colours, the pleasure-park rides and the lighthouse shape are inferred. Mile One had a fire in 2013: kept out of the model, flagged for the reviewer.
- `signatureRide` is still `danfo`; the dossier recommends `keke`.
- No hazard visual for flooding (no `hazardCues`), no real-phone test.
