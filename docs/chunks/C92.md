# C92 — Talk: bigger presets, polls, Board strip

Before typed chat (C93), the preset talk had to be worth using: 12 phrases is a greeting, not a conversation.

## What changed
- **Topic packs**: `places.json` has `topics` (Greet, Chat, Market, Food, Football, Music, Transport, Our area, Our state, Campaign talk, Banter, Weekend) and 149 phrases in English and Pidgin, up from 12. The Say tray shows the topics as chips, a **Recent** row of the last eight you said (kept on the device), and **Polls**. The server accepts any listed phrase id and nothing else.
- **Room polls**: pick a poll from Say, then Polls, and the room sees a card with the question and two or three options. Everyone votes once, sees live counts (never who voted for what), and it closes by itself after 45 seconds, then the result stays up for a moment. One poll open per room; a player waits 90 seconds before starting another. Starting a poll counts as saying something for the day's jobs and pays nothing itself. The starter's poll is hidden for anyone who muted them. Someone joining mid-poll gets it with what they already voted.
- **Board strip**: the Board shows "Most active in Mobilise today" (top three states) from `GET /api/places/activity`, which was built in C82 and had no screen. It renders nothing while offline or before any activity.
- **Fix found on the way**: in a tray with a long list, the rows above it (topic chips, reactions) were squashed to nothing and the list sat on top of them. Rows now keep their height and the tray scrolls.

## Content rules (tested)
Every phrase belongs to a known topic, each topic has at least eight, no repeats within a topic, both languages, 60 characters at most, no digits, links or `@`, and no Contender or party name in any phrase or poll. Poll and phrase ids never collide.

## Tests
`packages/content/test/places.test.ts`, `api/test/placesHub.test.ts` (open, busy, cooldown, one vote each, counts without who, closes by itself, joiner gets the poll), `api/test/places.test.ts` (large list accepted, polls from outside refused, nothing off the list, jobs and caps), `apps/game/test/places.test.ts` (poll events), `apps/game/e2e/places.spec.ts` (start, vote, count moves, no second vote).

## Not done
- **Pidgin needs a native reviewer** before launch. The lines are plain and short but were not checked by a native speaker.
- The Board strip has no automated browser test (it needs activity data from a confirmed account).
- Polls are per room and are not stored: nothing is kept after they close.
