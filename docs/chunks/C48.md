# C48 — a Convoy Yard that explains itself
Status: done      Depends on: C07, C44, C47
## Goal
Make the Yard readable. It is the only economy screen in the game, and it named no source for any
number on it: two currencies, five priced upgrades, a streak, a donation and seven gated rides, all
presented as bare figures. Also fix the loading screen, which rendered its text against the left
edge of the viewport instead of in the middle.
## Done when
Every figure on the Yard says where it comes from and what it buys, every number it prints is read
off the same content the run applies, and the three wallet tiles are one row of equal, aligned
cards.

## What was wrong

**Nothing said where either currency came from.** Funds and Following are the two things the whole
screen runs on. Following has exactly one source — one per supporter delivered to a venue, added on
the result screen — and the Yard never said so, which makes "20000 Following" on the Danfo card a
number with no action attached to it.

**The upgrades were unjudgeable.** A row was a name, a sentence and five dots. "Horn recharges
faster" did not say faster than what, by how much, how much of it you had already bought, or what
the dots counted. Nothing on the screen could tell you whether ₦80 was a good price.

**And the shop could not have said, because the numbers were in three places.** `meta.json` held a
`step` per upgrade; the client's `runModifiers()` re-typed every one of them as a literal
(`levels.magnet * 250`), with the horn's base and floor existing *only* there; and the store
re-typed the payout step a third time (`1000 + st.upgrades.value * 50`). Printing an effect would
have meant a fourth copy.

**The rally streak was mislabelled and buys nothing.** It was called "Day streak" and counts
finished rallies, not days — two finished runs in one hour move it by two. Nothing in the game reads
it: no bonus, no unlock, no gate.

**The loading screen sat against the left edge.** `Loading road…` was a bare `<p>` inside
`.cr-screen--briefing`, which lays its children out in a 560-px centred column. The paragraph was
centred as a block; its text was left-aligned inside it, so on a laptop it appeared a third of the
way across the screen and on a phone against the left margin.

## Shipped

**Every upgrade declares its effect in content.** `meta.json` gains an `effect` per upgrade —
`kind` (`mm-up`, `ticks-up`, `ticks-down`, `permille-up`), the `stat` it moves, a `label`, a `base`,
and a `floor` for the subtractive one — plus a top-level `maxLevel` and
`rewards.followingPerDelivered`. `packages/content/src/yard.ts` turns a level into a number
(`upgradeValue`), into the five run modifiers (`runModifiers`), into a rally payout (`runPayout`),
and into the string the shop prints (`upgradeReadout`). The client's copies are gone: `meta.ts`
re-exports the content functions and the store calls `runPayout`. A test walks every upgrade at
every level and asserts the shop's figure is the one `runModifiers` hands the sim.

**So the shop can now say what a level does.** Each row reads `Horn recharge 12.0s → 10.5s`,
`Pickup reach +0.25m → +0.5m`, `Load speed +0% → +6%`, with `Level 1 of 5` beside the pips and the
price on the right. An upgrade you cannot afford says `₦100 short` rather than greying out.

**The wallet tiles say their source**: `₦60 a rally, ₦4 a supporter` · `1 a supporter delivered` ·
`Rallies finished in a row`.

**A fourth tab, How it works**, states the lot: what Funds are and what they buy, what Following is
and that delivering is its only source, that Crowd is a score and is spent on nothing, a line-by-line
table of what one finished rally pays, and — plainly — that the rally streak buys nothing yet.
Every figure on the tab is interpolated from `metaConfig`, so it cannot describe a game the code is
not playing.

**The Rides tab explains the gate** in the unit the player can act on: a progress bar to the next
ride and "about 150 more supporters delivered" on each locked card.

**The Goals tab says what a goal pays** (₦70 daily, ₦180 weekly), that progress only moves on a
*finished* rally, that the ₦90 is a free daily donation, and when each list resets.

**The top row is three equal tiles on a shared grid.** `repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr))` so the widest
figure cannot take more width than the others, and each tile spans a three-row `subgrid`, so label,
figure and source line share a baseline across all three however differently their text wraps.
Measured equal to the pixel at 360, 390 and 1280 wide, with a flex fallback under
`@supports not (grid-template-rows: subgrid)`. The "next ride" progress moved out of the Following
tile into its own full-width bar underneath, which is what let the tiles get short enough to match.

**The loading screen is its own screen** (`.cr-screen--loading`) with a spinner, centred on both
axes — measured dead centre (0 px off) at 390×844 and 1280×800 — and `role="status"` so it is
announced. The spinner stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Tests

Seven new content tests, all of them about claims the screen now makes out loud:

- every upgrade owns its own stat, so no two fight over one modifier;
- no level is sold that does not move its number (catches a `ticks-down` upgrade reaching its floor
  mid-ladder and charging for dead levels);
- the shop's figure equals `runModifiers` at every level of every upgrade;
- the payout the How it works tab quotes is the payout the store pays, and the Fundraising Desk is
  the only upgrade that touches it;
- delivering is the only source of Following;
- every ride gate is reachable — at best-case deliveries on the best ride already open, no rung
  needs more than 150 runs;
- a day of goals plus the donation covers the cheapest upgrade, so the shop is not a price list on
  day one.

312 tests (was 305) and 81 (was 74); lint, typecheck, size-check and the e2e suite green.

## Open

- **The rally streak still buys nothing.** C48 made the screen say so rather than invent a bonus
  nobody asked for. Giving it an effect is a design decision, not a copy fix.
- The horn's `floor` (180 ticks) is never reached: five levels at 90 take 720 down to 270. It is a
  guard on a content change, and a test asserts no level is dead so it cannot quietly start biting.
- Nothing explains Crowd, stars or tiers on the Yard beyond one line — that belongs on the result
  screen, which is pre-C38 chrome.
- Still measured in Chrome, never played on a phone.
