## 3. Built form
Akure: a dense old core around the palace and market, newer quarters (Alagbaka, Avenue) with large bungalows; zinc roofs, painted block, churches. Idanre: old hilltop town of mud walls under rust-brown iron sheets, with a modern town below. Ilaje coast: stilt houses over water (**thin**; not described for Igbokoda specifically).

## 4. Road
Hilly asphalt through cocoa farms, laterite shoulders; river crossings (Owena, **no source for the bridge**); garages at the edge of Akure. **Okada** allowed by day, barred at night (7 pm–6 am from March 2026) and from named roads; keke and buses are common (**thin**: no keke source).

## 5. Vegetation, terrain, water, light and season
Rainforest belt with cocoa (pods, drying mats), yam and cassava, oil palm; two granite domes (Idanre) rising from the plain; lagoon with mangroves and canoes at Igbokoda; rain nearly ten months a year, wettest September.

## 6. Dress
Yoruba city dress: buba and iro wrapper, gele or head tie for women; men's agbada or shirt and trousers; aso-oke for ceremony. **Thin** for Ondo specifically (the Ogun and Oyo sets are the base). Avoid: a chief's or Oba's crown, beaded regalia, and white-robed religious dress.

## 7. Signs and language
Yoruba and English; Ilaje and Akoko variants exist but boards are English and Yoruba. Board words: COCOA, KOLA, YAM, FISH, POS. Yoruba tone marks need a native check.
