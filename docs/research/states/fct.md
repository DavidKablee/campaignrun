## 3. Built form
- **Contemporary city (the whole of the game's FCT):** a planned capital. Wide dual carriageways with grass medians, large roundabouts, mid-rise blocks in pale concrete and render with glass-blue bands, a few taller slabs, landscaped verges. Roofs are flat. Single-source (2).
- **Estates and satellite towns:** planned estates of tiled villas and bungalows behind walls; satellite towns with dense flat-roofed shopfronts and minibuses. Single-source (general knowledge, no search backing).
- No vernacular or colonial layer is drawn: the game shows the city, not the old villages around it.

## 4. Road
Dark asphalt, painted lane lines, grass medians, generous shoulders in pale grey, lamp posts, bus shelters. Roundabouts and flyovers are the city's signature. Minibuses and taxis are the visible public transport. Okada and keke are restricted in central districts (4, 5), so the kerbside shows few of them, mostly in the satellite town.

## 5. Vegetation, terrain, water, light and season
Savanna grassland and rocky hills at the edge of the city, with neem, mango, eucalyptus and shea trees in the verges. Jabi Lake is a man-made reservoir (3). Harmattan haze in the dry season is already a game weather mood. The ground is lawn green near parks and dry tan elsewhere.

## 6. Dress
A mix of office wear and everyday wear: tees, kaftans, riga, trousers, jeans, shoes and sneakers; women in blouses, ankara shirts, wrappers and jeans; head ties, a few hijabs. Colours stay muted. **Avoid list:** any colour block that matches a party; any cap, sash, banner or uniform that reads as a rally or a security force.

## 7. Signs and language
English first. Hausa shop words appear in the north of the territory. Boards used: PRINT · COPY, FRESH PRODUCE, PHARMACY, PURE WATER, POS · AIRTIME, PROVISIONS. A native check is needed on whether any board reads wrong.
