## 3. Built form
Old: mud courtyard houses with hipped roofs (historical; no evidence for what is standing today). Contemporary: block bungalows, storey houses and plazas along the Ring Road corridor; markets of lock-up shops. The moat appears as a green tree-lined bank with streets cut through it. **Thin**: no source describes present-day streets.

## 4. Road
Asphalt, many failed sections (2024), red-earth shoulders. The Ring Road is a circular road (about 25 km), where Akpakpava, Airport Road, Sapele Road and Forestry meet; flyovers are under construction (do not draw as finished). Yellow-roof buses, taxis and keke only in zones; **okada banned in the metropolis**.

## 5. Vegetation, terrain, water, light and season
Rainforest edge: oil palm, rubber tree rows, plantain, cassava; hills (Okpekpe, Somorika) on the horizon. Red laterite soil is stated for the state's hills in the draft but not for the city in a source: use it moderately. Flood-prone in the wet season (September peak), harmattan haze December–January.

## 6. Dress
City dress; women in wrapper and buba, men in shirts and trousers. Edo-specific marks (**thin**, lifestyle sources only): beaded collar (ewu-ivie), striped wrapper cloth, cap with beads. **Avoid**: red coral and ivory beads in full (the Oba's regalia), any chief's cap or red hat.
