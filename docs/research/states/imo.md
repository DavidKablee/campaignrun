## 3. Built form
Owerri: block and zinc buildings, many hotels and plazas, a civic centre in New Owerri, a hotel-and-nightlife city. Town edges: Igbo compounds with zinc roofs (**thin**; no Owerri house source; the only figure is a national 70.7% zinc roofs). Draw bungalows with zinc roofs and veranda rails, block shops and hotel blocks.

## 4. Road
Asphalt through red earth; named roads (Douglas, Wetheral, Tetlow, Works are colonial names and are not used on signs). Ride: **the municipality bans okada (about 18 years) and restricts keke (15+ years)**; buses are the main transport. Kupi still calls the yellow keke "most common", which probably describes the wider state. This contradicts the plan's "Imo stays keke" in the capital; see the plan.

## 5. Vegetation, water, light
Oil-palm bush (the rainforest is largely gone), cassava and farm plots, Oguta Lake with farmland and palm banks, canoes and pontoons. Heavy rain squalls July–Sep, dry in December.

## 6. Dress
City dress (shirts, wrappers, blouses) plus a few Igbo pieces: isiagu with a striped cap for men, blouse, wrapper and head tie for women, coral beads only on ceremony figures (the Anambra set already has isiagu). General Igbo sources only — **no Imo-specific street source**. No red-cap or party colours.
