## 3. Built form
Colonial admin core on the south bank (Government House, secretariat, old GRA), commercial Wurukum and Wadata, a university north bank. Houses: block bungalows with zinc roofs; in villages round mud huts with conical thatch. **Thin**: no Makurdi street-level source.

## 4. Road
Asphalt trunk roads (Makurdi–Otukpo–Enugu, –Lafia–Jos, –Yandev–Calabar, –Naka–Okene, –Katsina-Ala–Wukari); the Old Bridge carries trains and one road lane; the 1978 dual-carriageway bridge beside it; a new overhead bridge under construction at Wurukum. Keke and okada plus buses; no commercial taxis. Hot, with harmattan.

## 5. Vegetation, terrain, water, light and season
A wide brown-green river with sandbars, canoes and net fishers; savanna with scattered trees (mango is listed as a crop; shea is **unsourced**); grass plains; low ranges to the south-east; yam fields and barns. Rain April–October, harmattan haze Nov–Jan, floods in heavy seasons.

## 6. Dress
Tiv: black and white A'nger wrapper to the knee, elaborate versions for elders and festivals. Idoma: red and black (one weak source). Everyday: shirts, trousers, ankara. **Avoid**: red or green party hues in full outfits, a chief's headdress.

## 7. Signs and language
English, Hausa (Wadata), Tiv, Idoma, Igede. Board words: YAM, RICE, SOYA, BUKA, KEKE PARK. Tiv and Idoma text needs native speakers.
