## 3. Built form
A dense working city: lock-up shops, multi-storey plazas, factories, workshop sheds, zinc roofs, painted block walls in cream, green and brown. Sprawl, generator sheds and open drains. **Thin**: nothing describes Aba's houses; draw block bungalows and storey blocks.

## 4. Road
Asphalt, often flooded or broken in the wet season (Ngwa Road, Obohia, Uratta, Port Harcourt Road), drains and a canal on Ngwa Road, roundabouts being rebuilt. **Okada banned** (2023 and 2026 reports); **keke** barred 7 pm–6 am, buses and minibuses present, tricycle status in the 2026 ban unclear.

## 5. Vegetation, terrain, water, light and season
Flat low-lying plain, oil palm and raffia at the edges, the Aba River on the east side, wet most of the year (about 2,700 mm), short dry season December–January. Azumini Blue River: clear water, white sand, canoes, a hill view.

## 6. Dress
Business city dress: shirts, trousers, wrappers, head ties, work aprons for shoe and garment workers. Igbo pieces: isiagu and striped cap (already used by Anambra and Imo), Akwete cloth (Ukwa East weaving, a real local weave). **Thin**: no Aba-specific street source. Avoid: red caps or full coral sets (title regalia).

## 7. Signs and language
