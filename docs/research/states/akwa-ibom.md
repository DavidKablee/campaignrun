## 3. Built form
Uyo: a planned city of wide roads and roundabouts, civic block buildings, hotels and a mall, a large stadium. Villages: compound houses; materials not found (**thin**). The only reachable facts are generic: earth and compressed-block walls, thatch and palm for older buildings, corrugated iron for colonial and modern roofs. Draw block and zinc with a few raffia-thatch sheds.

## 4. Road
Wide asphalt, planted medians, roundabouts, drain edges; flooding after rain (#2). Ride: okada is banned in all of Uyo; keke is banned on named roads (Wellington Bassey Way, Abak, Aka, Ikot Ekpene, Oron) but one commentary says Uyo roads are "full of tricycles". Keke is therefore **real but partly restricted**; the recommendation keeps it (see plan). Keke colour: not found (**thin**).

## 5. Vegetation, water, light
Humid rainforest belt, oil palm, raffia stands, nipa and mangrove on the coast, long Atlantic beach, heavy rain (about 2.4 m/yr), humid haze; the dry spell is December.

## 6. Dress
Ceremonial Ibibio dress is documented (see #10); everyday Uyo dress is not. Plan: city dress (shirts, wrappers, blouses), with a few wrapper-and-matching-blouse sets and one bead necklace; no coral-bead chief regalia, no cane, no head pieces. **Annang-specific and everyday dress is thin.** No party colours.
