## 3. Built form
- **Old city:** mud-brick Hausa compounds round courtyards, flat roofs, relief ornament (same Hausa form as Kano and Katsina; **thin** for Bauchi itself). The mud walls are gone; the nine gates survive as rebuilt, widened gateways on the old wall line, with buildings crowding the line.
- **Hills:** nine rock hills stand inside and around the town. Kobi has houses at its foot and a reservoir on top (single source). They read as bare granite-like outcrops rising from flat ground.
- **Palace:** tall-walled mud; scenery only, unlabelled.
- **Railway/colonial:** railway town on the Port Harcourt–Maiduguri line (**thin**: no station detail found).
- **Modern:** block shops, plaza blocks, a pale stadium.

## 4. Road
Asphalt with sand shoulders and dusty verges (**thin**). Keke is the local taxi. Okada was banned in 2020; the state handed out 345 keke (#8, #9). Keke colour for Bauchi: not found (**thin**); use the national yellow-and-cream Napep convention with a Bauchi variant, `verified:false`.

## 5. Vegetation, terrain, light
Sudan-Guinea savanna: baobab and neem lines, acacia, sparse grass; rock hills on the town edge; Yankari's sandstone country is 100+ km south-central and must **not** be the city skyline. Warm spring under a cliff in the reserve. Dry-season haze late Oct to Feb.

## 6. Dress
Hausa-Fulani: men in a long gown (babban riga) or plain tunic with a cap (hula) and sometimes a turban; women in a wrapper (zani) with a matching blouse and a hijab or scarf, often dark or indigo. General Hausa sources only (Guardian Nigeria, RefinedNG, Legit); **no Bauchi-specific source** — thin. Avoid party colours and political-cap styling.

## 7. Signs and language
