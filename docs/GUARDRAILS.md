# Guardrails

These are non-negotiable legal and design constraints for Campaign Run.

1. **Disclosure**: Lives on the About screen, in the terms, and on payment screens and ad pages (owner decision, C79: it is not repeated on the splash, share cards, result or board screens, where it read as low confidence. C86 exception: the Board carries one plain line, "A game board", because it is the one screen that looks like a result; it is a label, not the disclosure text). Text: "Fictional game. All characters, parties and events are invented; any resemblance to real people is coincidental. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any political party, aspirant or electoral body. Scores are game scores, not polls."
2. **Not a poll**: Score is Crowd; the community view is the Rally Map. No polling-style percentages until 200 uniques per state.
3. **Parody names are allowed** when they joke about a role and would not make a stranger name one real person. **Never in the game:** party logos, symbols or slogans; real politicians' names or nicknames; phonetic echoes of those names; signature outfits, props, or faces; any character→person mapping; electoral-body marks; the player committing vote-buying/rigging/violence; weapons or injuries; insecurity incidents; ethnic/religious jokes; health/age/disability jokes; arrests; invented quotes from real people; home-state or ethnic bonuses.
4. **Villains are generic**: Integrity-threat characters never wear party colours, are never tied to a state/ethnicity/religion, and appear with equal frequency everywhere.
5. **Equal power**: All 6 Contenders are statistically equal (verified by 10k run bot).
6. **Money must not buy the map**: Real-money items are cosmetic/convenience. Map points are tier-capped.
7. **No paid randomness**: No loot boxes. No cash-out, no P2P transfers.
8. **No political ads**: No ads targeted by Contender choice.
9. **18+ Only**: Enforced on the splash screen: the first-time button reads "Run my race" and the line under it says tapping it confirms you are 18 or over (C82; the button used to carry the words). Sign-up repeats it.
10. **Contender choice is sensitive data**: Private by default. NEVER sent to server in R0 (until C11 consent).
11. **Palaces are scenery only**: Never a pickup zone or rally venue (implies traditional ruler endorsement).
12. **Hashed blocklist**: Real names are blocked via SHA-256 hashes. Plain text lists NEVER committed.
13. **Mobilise talk is presets, plus typed chat that ships dark** (C82, C93): by default players in a shared place can say only a phrase or emoji from `places.json` and the server rejects anything else, so nothing a player types reaches another player. Typed chat (C93) exists in the code but is off unless the server sets `CR_CHAT_ENABLED=true`, and it must stay off in production until a lawyer has reviewed the Cybercrimes Act and data-protection exposure, `pnpm --filter @cr/api report chat` is in use, and the word filter has been tested with native English and Pidgin speakers. When on, it is for an account at least 24 hours old with one finished job; a message is at most 80 characters, one per 4 seconds, with no links, handles, emails, phone or account numbers, floods or repeats; the hashed word filter (guardrail 12, plus `chat-terms.hashes.json`) drops names and abuse; a refused message is shown to nobody; five refusals in ten minutes, or reports from three different people, hide that player's text from others (they still see their own) for a while; a message is relayed live and kept 24 hours only so a report can be reviewed, then deleted (`place_chat`); the privacy copy says so. Turning the switch off falls back to the presets at once. Avatars are plain citizens seeded from the account unless the player has said yes (C89) to showing their Contender in Mobilise: that choice is asked once, defaults to private, is stored server-side (`users.mobilise_contender_id`), is separate from the map-colouring consent, can be withdrawn in Account, and only then does presence carry `look.contenderId` (the client can never send it). A shown Contender wears the character look and a party-colour sash only: no logos, symbols, slogans or catchphrases (guardrail 3), and locals never wear party colours (guardrail 4). The Studio look is never sent. Rewards are coins and a small Following head start, capped per Lagos day on the server; they never touch the sim or a Contender's power. Ambient "Locals" are labelled as such, never speak and are never counted as players. An invite link carries only a place (and optionally a room number), nothing about the player or their Contender. Rooms (C90): the room browser shows places and counts, never names; friends are made by tapping a player in your room (no typed names, no search), a friend sees where you are only once you both accepted, a block removes the link both ways and looks to the blocked person like a request nobody answered, and mutes are kept on the device by board name. The national square (`naija:square`) belongs to no state and adds nothing to a state's Board. Campaign Jobs and the streak (C91): the day's jobs come from the date alone, progress is counted on the server from accepted actions, rewards go through the same daily caps as an action, nothing is random or for sale, and a title is chosen from a fixed list (nobody types one). Talk (C92): phrases are grouped into topic packs and every phrase and poll is preset text in `places.json` (a content test keeps names of Contenders and parties, numbers and links out); a poll is started from that fixed list, one open per room, closes by itself, and shows counts, never who voted for what.
