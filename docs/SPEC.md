# Campaign Run — Spec (condensed v3 + amendments)

Full gameplay reference for agents. Legal guardrails: `GUARDRAILS.md`. Amendments: `SPEC-AMENDMENTS.md`.

## Product

Lane-based runner: pick a fictional Contender, drive a Nigerian state, load supporters at Pickup Zones, dodge hazards, unload at Rally Venues. Crowd score. 36 states + FCT unlock over Mondays; closes 15 Jan 2027 23:59 WAT.

## Core loop (75–100 s)

1. Border Gate → welcome.
2. 5 segments: Pickup Zone (brake ~1 s to load) → drive → Rally Venue (brake to unload).
3. Grand Rally → finish.
4. Result: skillCrowd (tiers/map later), displayCrowd (celebration).

## Controls

Swipe lanes, flick hop, hold Gas/Brake, Horn, Perk. Desktop: ←/→, ↑ gas, ↓ brake, Space hop, H horn, R radio, P pause, M mute.

## Failure

3-Dent: hit → −1 dent, drop ~25% carried, slow. 3 dents = breakdown (keep delivered Crowd).

## Contenders (original fiction)

Baba Bullabaa, Mr Tiko, Mr Obiliki, Comrade Yale, Mr. Don, Prof. Sarah. Perks are **in-run only**. Choice local until C11.

## Rides

Trek → Okada → Keke → Hilux → Danfo → Coaster → Grand Convoy. Capacity + Following gate + ₦.

## Calendar

R0 Mon 19 Oct: Lagos only. Then weekly unlocks; FCT 4 Jan; Final Week 11–15 Jan; close 15 Jan.
